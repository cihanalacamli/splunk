Splunk & Cisco 360

Why this matters for Cisco partners: Splunk is not only a product conversation. It also becomes a Cisco 360 portfolio motion with its own value-index logic, enablement path and growth levers.

Portfolio view Splunk has its own Value Index Splunk is treated as a separate Cisco portfolio value index. Unlike other portfolios, it currently focuses on Performance, Capabilities and Engagement rather than Foundational.
Capabilities Enablement and badges matter Partners can improve differentiation through foundational enablement and Partnerverse badges, including Advanced, Solutions, Services Capability and Industry badges.
Performance Growth is measured Splunk growth levers include incremental ACV, new customer sales, license capacity growth in existing customers and TCV growth across the Cisco portfolio.
Engagement Source, market and document Partner-sourced business, co-marketing activity and documented customer success can all support the Splunk practice growth story.
What partners should do next
  • Complete Splunk foundational enablement.
  • Identify which Partnerverse badges fit the practice.
  • Use partner-sourced opportunity discipline.
  • Track renewals for cross-sell and upsell potential.
  • Document customer success stories early.
Where Ingram Micro can help
  • Explain Splunk Value Index levers in plain language.
  • Help partners prioritize enablement and badges.
  • Support account mapping for iACV and TCV growth.
  • Guide renewal, deal-registration and customer-success motions.
  • Help partners interpret Cisco 360 impact through PXP / DPV where access is available.
Note: Cisco states that the Cisco + Splunk partner opportunity is about more than product integration: partners can engage with both programs, access resources, training and support, and expand into new markets.
