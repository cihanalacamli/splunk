For partners

Partner activation journey

A cleaner flow from eligibility to activation. The goal is simple: make partners understand how to enter, what to learn first and how to build the first opportunity with Ingram Micro.

STEP 1

Establish eligibility

A company must first be a Cisco partner before it can move into the Splunk partner journey.

Required⌄
Cisco partner prerequisiteBefore anything else, the company must hold the right Cisco partner foundation. Start by aligning with Ingram Micro Türkiye.
Ingram Micro alignmentConfirm company profile, main contacts and intended Splunk motion with the Ingram Micro team.
Why this step mattersIt prevents partners from entering the Splunk journey without the proper Cisco ecosystem foundation.
STEP 2

Apply to Splunk Partnerverse

After Cisco partner eligibility is in place, continue with the official Splunk partner application flow.

Official⌄
Become a PartnerStart from the official Splunk partner page.
Partner SignupUse the Splunk Partner Signup portal to begin the formal application.
Portal Registration WorkflowOfficial onboarding guidance will be expanded after Cisco & Splunk distributor alignment.
Partnerverse remains the current Splunk partner program framework. Splunk states this structure is planned to fully integrate into Cisco 360 during 2027.
STEP 3

Choose the first motion

Select the Splunk motion that matches the customer pain: Security, Observability or Data Platform.

Motion⌄
SecurityStart here for SOC modernization, SIEM, detection, investigation and response use cases.
ObservabilityStart here for application performance, outage analysis, MTTR and service-health conversations.
Data PlatformStart here for machine data, operational intelligence and AI-ready data stories.
STEP 4

Assign the role path

Once the motion is selected, split the journey into Sales and Technical paths.

Role⌄
Sales PathBuyer pain, use-case positioning, discovery questions, competition awareness and first pipeline action.
Technical PathData sources, architecture, integrations, demo storyline, training path and technical validation.

STEP 6

Build the first opportunity

Map measurable pain, identify the buyer and turn the first use case into a pipeline conversation.

Pipeline⌄
Account signalsAlert fatigue, recurring outages, visibility gaps, compliance pressure, data sprawl and AI-system uncertainty.
Discovery promptsWhich incidents take longest to investigate? Which business service is most sensitive to downtime? Where do teams lack context?
Qualification ruleMap one measurable pain, one owner, one usable data source and one next action before going deeper.

Use Ingram Micro Türkiye to move from learning into workshops, architecture guidance, GTM and pipeline support.
